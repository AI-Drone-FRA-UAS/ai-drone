Tools, five recordings and a [listen page](artifacts/runtime-tone-20260909/listen.html) are in `artifacts/runtime-tone-20260909/` (local; `artifacts/` is Git-ignored).

- **Motors at runtime — works, verified.** ArduPilot's tone alarm plays a MAVLink `PLAY_TUNE` MML string through the motor windings, separate from the AM32 startup melody. Needs `NTF_BUZZ_TYPES` bit 1 and `SERVO_DSHOT_ESC=1`, both read at boot, so an FC-only reboot is required. Proven by A/B: the same tune gave 2,410 ms of sound routed, and complete silence at baseline. Limits are structural — `Util::toneAlarm_set_buzzer_tone` quantizes pitch to five DShot beacon commands and sends them to `ALL_CHANNELS`, so it is five pitches, mono across all four ESCs, and dropped while armed. Rhythm and tempo are free. Only O4 C-D-E-F-G reaches all five beeps; MMLPlayer's octave counter is not scientific pitch notation, its O4 C is 1046.5 Hz. `artifacts/runtime-tone-20260909/tone_paths.py` reproduces the mapping with tests pinned to firmware source.
- **Flight-controller buzzer (PD15 pad) — dead end, proven.** Reboot recordings at `NTF_BUZZ_TYPES` 3, 1 and 0 are identical, so nothing audible comes from that pin; the boot sound is the ESCs, its late burst matching the documented AM32 `playInputTune()` harmonics. The board declares `PD15 BUZZER OUTPUT GPIO(80) LOW`, a plain GPIO — the timer variant is commented out because the LED strip uses that timer's DMA — and only `ToneAlarm` implements `play_tune`, while this build carries `#undef HAL_PWM_ALARM`. Even with a buzzer fitted it could only play 100 ms on/off patterns from ArduPilot's own events.
- **Everything else is silent:** MTF-01P, MT-15, IMX500 camera, M100 GPS, ELRS receiver. The 4-LED NeoPixel strip on `SERVO9` is the only visual accompaniment, and this build has no Lua scripting (no `SCR_` parameters) to sync it. Real polyphonic music needs a piezo on a free Pi hardware-PWM GPIO (BCM13/18/19; 12 is the payload servo, 14/15 the FC UART) or an I2S DAC — no audio device is installed on the Pi, and adding one is a hardware decision for the user.

**Conclusion for melody work:** the ESCs remain the only instrument. Per-ESC AM32 melodies are the only four-voice path and stay boot-only; the DShot tone alarm is the only on-command path and is five pitches in unison.

**Safety:** follow `AGENTS.md`; props removed, disarmed, battery power for ESC access. No arm, throttle, motor-test, servo or flight-mode commands. Inspect sound changes with the microphone; do not silence flight warnings. Last completed tone session left maintenance disabled and the FC disarmed.
