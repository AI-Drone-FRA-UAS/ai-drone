145 ms for the first middle C and 120 ms for the second, separated by a
69 ms programmed pause. The pause after the second is approximately 94 ms.
Other sounded notes have approximately 34 ms articulation gaps. Channel 2's
lower voice is now restricted to the two F accents, at slots 0 and 12, to
reduce the strong upper harmonics masking the middle passage. These are

the broadband RMS dip increased from 0.23 dB to 4.99 dB below the mean hit

separation, but does not establish an exact subjective sound match. Latest
sampled pitch error is approximately 17 cents and the onset-derived tempo
estimate is approximately 138 BPM. The target remains 140 BPM; onset estimates
are affected by the ensemble and room resonance. No recording was pitch-shifted
or time-stretched.

## Sound after the melody

The short following sound matches AM32's `playInputTune()` input-ready
confirmation, generated by the ESCs through the motor windings. It is separate
from the flight-controller GPIO buzzer. The matching firmware's
[input-ready path](https://github.com/am32-firmware/AM32/blob/d621bf2a2be1ed4b4ac9597df089f0727edbc67c/Src/main.c#L1361-L1401)
plays it after valid input has stayed at zero for approximately one second.
ESC input-readiness does not mean the flight controller has been armed.

The fixed [sound routine](https://github.com/am32-firmware/AM32/blob/d621bf2a2be1ed4b4ac9597df089f0727edbc67c/Src/sounds.c#L193-L211)
sets PWM dividers 80, 70 and 40 for 100 ms each. With the target's timer constants,
the nominal fundamentals are approximately 296, 338 and 585 Hz. Recorded strong
harmonic peaks around 2,074, 2,035 and 1,761 Hz agree with the expected 7th, 6th
and 3rd harmonics. Its three stages and roughly one-second delay after the
melody provide additional evidence for this identification.

In the inspected installed-version source, this sequence is hardcoded. The
128-byte startup-melody EEPROM field does not control it. Changing just that
confirmation sequence requires modifying, building and flashing appropriate
ESC firmware. The beep-volume setting is shared with other ESC sounds, so it
does not independently control this confirmation. No such firmware or volume
change was made.
